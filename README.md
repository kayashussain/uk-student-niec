# International Student Recruitment (ISR)

ISR is a recruitment portal that any education consultancy can use: the student sheet, student cards, messages, university Fact File, partners and agents, and a Dashboard. The name of the consultancy is a setting. This first installation is for Nepal International Educational Consultancy.
One login for everyone; each person sees only what their account allows.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Student Details** (`student-details/`) | The sheet of students by intake (the page is called **All Students** in the menu), with live co-editing, the Dashboard, the Users and Activity pages (Admin), and the **Fact File** (one page per university: fees, entry requirements, English tests, courses, intakes and the application process, searchable by course). |
| **Today** (`today.html`, `today-page.js`) | The first screen after login for every account, kept quiet on purpose: a greeting, then **Your tasks** (a team) or **Tasks you have given** (Admin and the Manager), **For you** (a team's tasks; for Admin the tasks the team gave them), **Tasks you have given** and, for Admin and the Manager, **Given by others**; each says who gave it to whom ("SK Kayas Hussain → Application Team"), is ticked done right there and has its own comments; a red dot marks what is new for the reader (a comment from the other side, a task just given to them), with a red badge on Today in the menu on every page until it is read; then one **Needs attention** list, a line per kind of work with how many students wait (inquiries to follow up, applications to submit, mock interviews to schedule, offers waiting for the deposit, CAS received with the visa not lodged, ...), opened with a click to show who. No number tiles and no buttons (those are on the Dashboard and All Students). Only the students the account may see are shown. |
| **My dashboard** (`my-dashboard.html`, `my-dashboard-page.js`) | Each team's own Dashboard (Application, Mock, Follow-up, each branch): four figures in the team's own words, the team's tasks (open, done this week), **This week** against the week before, **Where the students are** by stage, **Coming up** in the next 7 days and **Good news** of the last 30 days. The Application, Mock and Follow-up teams' figures count the whole current intake (numbers only); names are only of the students the team sees. A team that opens /dashboard gets this page; Admin and the Manager keep the full Dashboard. |
| **Calendar and dates** (`shared/ui.js`) | Every date box opens an Apple-style calendar on a computer (the phone's own date wheel on a phone), and every date is written "25 Aug 2026" (the day first, the month in words). |
| **Student card** (`student.html`, `student-page.js`, `cards.js`, `portal-api.js`) | Opens from a double-click on a student's name, email, number, university, course or row number (a click on a date cell opens its calendar), and from the Fact File. Three tabs: **Notes** (plain notes about the student, no kinds to choose; the newest first), **University options** (the options chosen for the student, then a study level (Foundation, International Year 1, Top-up, Undergraduate, Pre-Masters, Postgraduate (MBA too), Masters by Research or PhD), a course name search and remarks, kept on the card; each course found has **Add** and **Open**, its university's page in the Fact File) and **Send a message** (six templates, each opening on the full-page composer). **+ Add student** asks, in this order: where the student starts (the stage); the student (first and last name, email, WhatsApp number, date of birth, passport number, address as in the passport, nationality; the passport details are needed from Application Stage on, optional at Inquiry); the student source (for Social Media: the platform, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp, then Organic or Advert, and for an advert the campaign from Events and marketing or its name under Other); and optional remarks. A student already in ISR (same phone or email) is never added twice: the form names them and offers Open their card or **Fill in what is missing**; the same phone under a clearly different first name (a brother or sister on the family phone) also offers **Add anyway (a different person)**, and an event list never links such a line on its own (it is shown as Check); which fills only blank details, tags the source when none was recorded, and keeps a different email or phone in a note (an application's email is never replaced). The date of birth (with the age when the student applied), passport number, address and nationality show in the card's top panel and change with Edit details; an empty one says **Missing** in orange, and from Application Stage on the student is listed on Today under **Passport details missing** (Admin, the Manager and the Application Team). Remarks and Student Source are not columns of the sheet any more (their data stays in the file).
| **Messages** (`messages.js`, `templates.html`, `compose.html`) | Every email ends with the office signature picture (sent inside the email). A consultancy puts its own `email-signature.png` in the data folder (`STUDENT_DETAILS_DATA_DIR`) and that picture is used; `student-details/email-signature.png` is only the first installation's starting picture. Six templates, and **Other** to write your own words: university options and documents, documents needed, application submitted, offer received, mock interview, deferred. Each opens on a full page (`/compose`): the email at full size, editable words, files to attach (read in the browser, sent once and never stored on the server), then Send, Open in my email program, Copy or WhatsApp (`wa.me`). The options email can carry a minimum cash deposit column. Email goes from the office mailbox set up in `mail.json`. Admin edits the templates and the signature.
| **University options and the Fact File assistant** (`course-index.js`, `eligibility.js`, `places.js`, `finder.js`, `fact-finder.js`) | Every course of the Fact File as one searchable list. Since 14 Nov 2026 the student card's University options is a plain course search by study level and name (the owner asked), so no page asks the assistant now; it stays on the server (`/api/finder`) and in the tests. It reads the student's grade, gap, English score, budget, level, subject, place ("near Manchester", "in Scotland", "within 20 miles of Leeds"; a built-in map of UK cities) and remarks ("5 universities", "show the minimum cash deposit and the full tuition fee"), judges every course against the entry rules written in the Fact File and lists who is eligible, likely eligible or ruled out, with fee, deposit, scholarship and link. It runs inside the server with no outside AI and no key.
| **Course pages and the weekly check** (`university-websites.js`, `course-finder.js`, `course-watch.js`) | The official website of each university is listed in `university-websites.js` (or written in the Fact File). Only those websites are searched or opened. A course with no page written down is searched for; every course page is opened about once a week (a slice every six hours, the first ten minutes after the server starts; `COURSE_WATCH=off` stops it). A page that is gone, no longer about the course, or whose fees or intake months changed becomes a **warning** on the Fact File (a line on the Fact File page, a list on the university's page) for a person to confirm. The check never edits the Fact File itself. Results are in `course-watch.json` in the data folder. |
| **My email and WhatsApp** (`user-mail.js`, `my-email.html`, `my-email-page.js`) | Each person sets up their own mailbox (address and password, checked with a test email, the password kept encrypted on the server) so the emails they send to students go from their own address, signed with their own name and team. A team member without one cannot email; Admin falls back to the office mailbox. WhatsApp always opens in the person's own WhatsApp. |
| **Follow-up** (`bulk.html`, `bulk-page.js`) | One message to many students at once, by email or WhatsApp, each with their own name and details. The students are chosen by intake, stage, **Source** (Social Media, Visitor, a branch, an agent, or exactly which platform or campaign) and name. |
| **Portal logins and signatures** (`partner-access.js`, `user-mail.js`, `my-email-page.js`) | The username and password of a university's or a partner's application portal are shown only to Admin and the Application Team (on Partner Universities, B2B Partners, Agents and the student card); everyone else sees the portal link. Each team member sets up their own email and uploads a signature picture (PNG or JPG, at most 1 MB) on **My email** (reached from **Message templates**, "Your email and signature"); their emails end with that picture, Admin's with the office signature. There is no typed signature form: Message templates has only a **Company** tab (the company name and short name used across ISR, and whether the office mailbox gets a copy of every email). The account menu holds only Log out. |
| **Growth, ups and downs, and where students live** (`dashboard-trends.js`, `nepal-map.js`, `/api/trends`) | On the Dashboard, in a balanced order (a chart, then a pair of plain cards, then a chart): the headline figures; **Ups and downs over time** (a chart like a stock market's: one line each for inquiries, applications, offers, payments, CAS and visas, by day, week or month, on the day each happened, a lead list's own inquiry days included; tap a name to show or hide its line; each line's peak marked; a crosshair reads every line; events and campaigns marked; a source filter narrows every line to the students of one source, where a student from an event, a campaign or a paid advert counts under Events and marketing; a few sentences on the last 4 weeks against the 4 before); **How students found us** and the visa target; **Business growth by intake** (two plain bar charts, students and visas in each intake with the numbers printed, one sentence on the last closed intake, and a table with each figure's change on the intake before and the **visa rate**, visas for every 100 students; the newest marked In progress); **Where students live in Nepal** (a map with a dot per district, read from the address in the passport; the students from outside Nepal by nationality; the age when they applied); then partners and the student journey, and universities. A round **up** button at the bottom right of every page goes back to the top once a page is scrolled. The map's shapes are from geoJSON-Nepal (MIT License). |
| **Tasks** (`cards.js`, `today-page.js`, `student-page.js`) | Admin and the Manager press **Assign a task** on a student card: choose the team (Application, Mock, Follow-up or a branch); a branch can give its own students' tasks to the Application or Mock Team; the Manager, every team and every branch can also give Admin a task (Admin is named with their name and the signature's title, for example "SK Kayas Hussain (Head of Recruitment)"); whoever gave a task sees it under **Tasks you have given** on Today and can remove it, and write what to do (no date to do it by, the owner, 14 Nov 2026). The team sees it under **Your tasks** on Today and ticks it complete (or not complete again). Each task has its own **comments**, like a chat: the team asks for what it needs (a student's email, a document) or says how it went, with or without ticking, and whoever gave the task answers; both see it on Today and on the student card. Notes on the card are about the student; the talk about a piece of work is in its task; while it is open, and for 7 days after, the team can open that student even if the student is not at its stage. Admin and the Manager see every task they gave under **Tasks you have given**, with who completed it. **Hand on**: the team a task is with, whoever gave it, Admin or the Manager hands it on to another team (or to Admin) in the same task, with a reason; the hand-on is a line in the comments ("Mock Team handed this to Application Team"), the route shows every team it went through ("SK → Mock Team → Application Team"), and the teams it was with still see it (under **Handed on by your team**) and comment; only the team it is with ticks it. Kept on the student's card (in the daily backups); every change is in Activity. |
| **Events and marketing** (`events.html`, `events-page.js`, `planning.js`) | Admin adds, the Manager reads. Every event or piece of marketing (a fair, a seminar, a Facebook advert, a signboard ...) has a name, where it ran (Social media, Our office, A college or school, Another venue, Newspaper/radio/TV, Online ads, Other; older entries are read from their old type), the days it ran, its cost (typed in rupees, US dollars or pounds; dollars and pounds are turned into rupees at the day's rate, from the same rate site as the Incentive page, and every total is in rupees) and a note. Each is a tag: choose it as the Student source on a student (one by one with Add student, or many with **Add many students**, offered inside Add student), and it shows on the student card with its dates. Which students came from an event or a campaign is settled by the list Admin uploads for it (open it on Events and marketing, Excel or CSV, up to 1,000 lines): every line is shown for review before anything is saved; new students go into the intake they asked for (a column such as "Intended UK Study Intake: January 2027" puts them in Jan Intake 2027), else the chosen intake, at Inquiry, with the source Social Media for a campaign on social media and Events for anything else, tagged with it; their names are tidied (stray symbols, capitals), a Nepal number is kept as 98XXXXXXXX, each column is read by what its heading means, so any list works (a Facebook lead export, an event's sign-up sheet, a campaign report): the inquiry date (12 November 2026, 12/11/2026, 2026-11-12 or an Excel date) and its time become the first inquiry day and time, shown on the card under Where the student came from; the programme becomes the study level; the programme, the intake asked for, comments and anything else about the student go into the first note; what is about the list, not the student (the inquiry or serial number, ids, links such as an inbox link, and the platform, campaign, ad and form names) is left out; a student already on the sheet (same phone, matched on its last 10 digits, or email, in any intake) and in no event is tagged with it instead of being added twice (the earlier source is kept in the remarks and a note goes on the card); a student already tagged with another event is left as it is and listed; a line with no name, or with neither a phone nor an email, is skipped; the same phone under another first name (a brother or sister) is shown as Check and not linked. A list that knows more about students already in ISR is never stopped (shown as **Update**): a blank email or phone is filled in (a different one goes into a note, the sheet's is kept), the first inquiry day becomes the earliest day known (so a lead added before the list had its days gets its real day), a blank study level is filled in, the time comes with the earlier day, and the comments or other details about the student go into a note ("More from the list of ..."), only the parts the card does not already say; a note an earlier version wrote by copying every column ("From the updated list of ...") is tidied away by the next upload; the same list uploaded again with nothing new changes nothing. Re-uploading is safe (the owner, 14 Nov 2026): the same person twice in one file is **Joined** into one line with the details of both; a lead that fits two different students in ISR (the phone one's, the email another's, or two students sharing a number) is **Check** and changes neither; a student removed from the sheet earlier is **Removed before** and never added back by a list (each card keeps the phone and email its list gave, and a card stays when its row is deleted); a cancelled, withdrawn or held student keeps their stage, a list only adds details. A campaign can still be picked under Social Media or Events when a student is added (Events needs the event). Each event opens as its own view with its figures (cost per student, cost per visa) and its students with their latest note; a click opens the student card. On All Students, **Source** shows only the students of one source (Social Media, Visitor, Reference, Language Classes, Events and marketing, Agents, Branches) or exactly which (a platform, an event or campaign, a branch, an agent), for every account and the students it sees. The name, email, phone, university, course and partner columns have their own filters too. The page warns how many social media students are on no campaign's list. An updated, longer list for the same event or campaign can be uploaded at any time: the people already tagged are recognised ("already linked", or "Update" when the list has something new about them) and the new ones are added; anyone tagged earlier who is not on the new list is named in an orange note in the review and left as they are (never removed). Excel files are read by ISR itself (`student-file.js`), so a sheet formatted as a table (as Meta exports leads) reads too; the Excel library is only a fallback. |

## Run it on your computer

```bash
npm start            # the site on http://localhost:8080 (Incentive at /incentive, set INCENTIVE_PASSWORD to open it)
```

The first time, the server writes a one-time setup code to `first-admin-code.txt` in its data folder (and prints it
in the log). Open `/login`, enter it, and create the owner (Admin) account. Needs Node 18 or newer; there is nothing
to install.

## Try it on your computer first

```bash
node scripts/local-demo.js     # made-up students and data, at http://localhost:8080 (log in as owner, password Demo-Password-2026)
```

Nothing real is in it and nothing leaves the computer (no email is sent). `--keep` starts it again on the demo folder as you left it.
Details and the other demo logins are at the top of `scripts/local-demo.js`.

## Tests

```bash
npm test
```

The rules, data and security checks run anywhere. The browser tests (`test/ui.test.js`: log in, edit, delete, search,
team and Manager rules, Fact File, Incentive, accessibility) run where Playwright and Chromium are installed
(`npm i -D playwright axe-core`) and skip themselves otherwise.

## Deploying and running it

- `CPANEL_DEPLOY.md`: Nest Nepal cPanel (the live setup).
- `DEPLOY.md`: Render or any server with `deploy.sh`.

Both explain accounts, backups, email and what is stored where. Data lives outside the code folder, so an update
never touches it. `/healthz` answers `{"ok":true}` for an uptime monitor.

## Claude Strategy Advisor connector (read-only)

Lets a Claude Project read the live sheet (every tab, row and column), the Dashboard numbers and the Fact File. It cannot
change any student or the Fact File. The only thing it can write is the Advisor's own notebook (`advisor-memory/` in the data
folder: preferences, how NIEC works, lessons; notes with emails or phone numbers are refused, and every save keeps a backup). It is off until you set `NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN` (a random secret of 32 or more characters) in the host
settings and restart. Then add this address in claude.ai (Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector):

```
https://<your-site>/advisor-mcp/<NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN>
```

Anyone with that address can read all students, so keep it private. To cut access off, remove or change the variable and restart.
Make a secret with `node -e "console.log(require('crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('hex'))"`.

### Incentive questions through the same connector

The connector above also answers questions about the **Incentive page**, with no second connector and no new address. After the
Student Details app restarts, Claude gets four more read-only tools: `get_incentive_overview`, `get_incentives` (every visa-issued
student with their **University Partner** (the agent), tuition fee, university commission and the incentives entered),
`get_payment_summary` (Payment Summary and Company Earnings of an intake) and `summarize_incentives_by` (totals by University
Partner, university or intake). They cannot change anything. Claude may need the connector refreshed in claude.ai to list them.

The Student Details server reads the Incentive page's saved figures (`commissions.json`, `advances.json`, `exchange-rate-cache.json`)
straight from the Incentive data folder. It finds that folder next to its own data folder (`.../niec_data/counselor-incentive`, the
layout in `CPANEL_DEPLOY.md` and `deploy.sh`), or set `COUNSELOR_DATA_DIR` on the Student Details app to the same value the
Incentive page uses. If the folder cannot be found, the incentive tools say so instead of showing zeros. Because it is one connector,
**the one secret opens both the students and the commissions**: keep the address private. Both connectors' shared code is in
`shared/mcp-core.js`.

## Daily backups and restore (Admin only)

Every day after 6 p.m. (office time, `APP_TIMEZONE`, default Asia/Kathmandu) the Student Details server saves one compressed
backup of the students, the Fact File and the Partner Universities in `daily-backups/` in the data folder, and deletes copies
older than 30 days. If the host has put the server to sleep, the copy is made at the first request instead. Accounts and passwords
are never in a backup. Admin restores from **Activity → Restore data** (students, Fact File and Partner Universities each on
their own); a restore first saves the current data as a "before restore" backup. **View** opens the real Student Details, Fact File and Partner Universities pages on that backup (`?backup=ID`, read only, nothing saved or polled), with a bar to switch between them and restore. Uploaded agreement files are not in the backup.

## Where things are

- `student-details/app.js`: the sheet. `facts.js` and `fact-*.js`: the Fact File. `fact-seed-*.js(on)`: the starting
  Fact File content (an unedited university page is refreshed from it; one that has been saved by Admin is never touched).
- `shared/teams.js`: who sees and changes what (used by the page and the server alike).
- `shared/accounts.js`: login, sessions, people.

## ISR: what changed in round 11

- **Name:** the portal is called International Student Recruitment, ISR for short, with the consultancy's logo (`brand-logo.png`) at the top of the menu and on the login page. The consultancy's own name is still a setting. The browser-tab and phone icons show the consultancy's initials in white on its logo blue (`student-details/favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`).
- **Student Source:** one **Branch** choice with a second dropdown for which branch (the list is `BRANCHES` in `student-details/card-fields.js`; add a branch there and it becomes a branch team, a Users page choice and a source everywhere). The sheet keeps "<name> Branch", so the incentive sums and the branch accounts work as before. Agent shows the agents, Events the events that were held, Language Classes the classes, Reference and Visitor their own lists, and Social Media asks for the platform and a remarks box (the campaign name). All forms use one component, `source-picker.js`.
- **Add many students:** from an Excel or CSV file only (the paste box is gone). Bad lines and students already on the sheet are skipped, with the reason.
- **Events and marketing:** new types for Meta (Facebook or Instagram) campaigns, campaigns on a partner page, and campus or college campaigns that are not schools.
- **B2B Partners and Agents:** a Commission column shows the current intake's commission as a PDF or link that opens when clicked, or a "Missing for this intake" button. Each intake's commission is set in the editor (PDF upload or link).
- **Fact File:** every course is a link to its own page, or shows "Find the course page".
- **Dashboard:** one calm page with only what the head of the consultancy needs (see the table above); the other tabs and the Intake plan are gone, and the visa target is set on the page.
- **Workspaces:** Admin, Manager, Application Team, Mock Team, Follow-up Team and each Branch have their own Today (their tasks and what needs attention), their own Dashboard (Admin and the Manager the full one) and menu, and a team sees its own sheet columns highlighted (`Teams.kindOf` in `shared/teams.js`).

## See your real data on your own computer

The students you see in `scripts/local-demo.js` are made up. To look at your real data without touching the live server: download a backup from the
live server (Admin, Restore, or the daily backup), then run `node scripts/local-live.js <the downloaded file>` and open http://localhost:8080. It opens straight into the portal as the owner with no login page, and only on that computer (add `--login` for the normal login page). The copy
is private to your computer (folder `live-data/`, never in git) and changes made there never reach the live server. Delete `live-data/` when you finish.
