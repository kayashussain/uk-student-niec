Daily backups

Every day after 6 p.m. the server saves a backup of the students, the Fact File and the Partner Universities, and keeps the last 30 days. Older ones are deleted automatically. People's accounts and passwords are not part of a backup.

Restore from a backup

Pick a day and press Restore. Before anything is changed, the data as it is right now is saved as a "before restore" backup, so a restore can be undone too. After a restore, ask everyone to refresh their page.
