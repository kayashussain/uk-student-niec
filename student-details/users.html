Add a person

They get an email with a link and a temporary password. At their first login they choose their own password and link an authenticator app on their phone.

Usernames are not case sensitive. Admin is the owner account and cannot be given to anyone.

People

When someone leaves, click Remove. Their login is deleted and they are logged out of every device at once. Disable pauses a login without deleting it.
